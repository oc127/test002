# 新海诚风格 - 黄昏电车告别

**核心概念**: 放学时分的东京郊区站台，穿校服的少女与朋友挥手告别，夕阳西下

## 流派

**新海诚派** (Makoto Shinkai Style)

参考作品：《你的名字》《言叶之庭》

## 视觉元素清单

- [x] 光粒子：樱花吹雪 + 夕阳中的尘埃
- [x] 天空占比：天空占画面 60%，粉橙晚霞
- [x] 情感定格：少女最后回头看
- [x] 发丝动态：长发被风吹起
- [x] 眼部高光：瞳孔反射夕阳

## 3 段式分镜

```
[前段 0-3s]  日常       少女站在站台，背对电车，长发在风中飘动
[中段 3-7s]  转折       她回头，看向站台另一端的朋友，樱花飘过
[尾段 7-10s] 余韵       电车驶近，她最后一次挥手，镜头上移到天空
```

## Prompts

### Kling（首推，日系审美识别度最高）

```
新海诚风格动漫。黄昏时分，东京郊外的电车站台。一位穿着海军蓝水手服
的高中女生站在月台边缘，长黑发被夏末的风轻轻吹起。天空占画面上方
三分之二，被夕阳染成粉橙色，云朵层次分明，阳光穿透云层形成可见的
光柱。樱花花瓣在空气中飘舞。相机从中景缓慢推进到女生的面部特写，
她的瞳孔中倒映着夕阳。赛璐璐角色 + 超写实水彩背景，静止的情感瞬间，
2.35:1 宽银幕电影感。
```

### Runway Gen-3

```
Makoto Shinkai anime style, "Your Name" aesthetic. Dusk at a suburban
Tokyo train station. A high school girl in navy sailor uniform stands
at the platform's edge, long black hair flowing in late summer breeze.
Sky dominates upper 2/3 of frame, painted in vibrant pink and orange
with layered cloud formations. Volumetric god rays piercing through
the clouds. Sakura petals drift through the air. Camera slowly pushes
from medium shot to close-up of her face, her eyes reflecting the
sunset. Cel-shaded characters against hyperrealistic watercolor
backgrounds. Still emotional moment. 2.35:1 cinematic widescreen.
```

### Sora（长 prompt 版，适合 10s+ 剧本）

```
A 10-second anime sequence in the style of Makoto Shinkai's films.

The scene opens at a quiet suburban Tokyo train station during
golden hour. The sky dominates the top 60% of the frame, painted
in layered pinks and oranges with dramatic cloud formations and
volumetric god rays piercing through. A few sakura petals drift
past the camera.

A high school girl stands on the platform in a navy blue sailor
uniform, her long black hair gently flowing in the warm evening
breeze. The camera slowly pushes in from a medium shot. She
slowly turns her head to look at something off-camera—her
expression is a mix of quiet sadness and acceptance. Her eyes
catch the sunset light in sharp anime-style highlights.

In the final beats, a train approaches from the left, its headlight
catching the petals in the air. The camera tilts up to the
brilliant sunset sky as the train's whistle sounds softly.

Style: cel-shaded characters against hyperrealistic painted
backgrounds, Makoto Shinkai's signature look from "Your Name"
and "Weathering With You". Anime aesthetic, 2.35:1 widescreen.
```
